Space missions

Missions that changed everything

Sixteen journeys — crewed and robotic, from four continents — that took us from the first satellite to the edge of the Solar System. Filter by destination to explore.

Soviet Union

1957

Sputnik 1

The first artificial satellite, a 58 cm metal sphere whose radio beeps launched the Space Age.

Soviet Union

1961

Vostok 1

Yuri Gagarin became the first human in space, completing one orbit of Earth in 108 minutes.

NASA

1969

Apollo 11

Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin became the first humans to walk on the Moon.

Soviet Union

1970

Venera 7

The first spacecraft to soft-land on another planet and transmit data from Venus.

NASA

1977

Voyager 1 & 2

Twin probes that toured the giant planets; Voyager 1 is now the most distant human-made object.

NASA / ESA

1990

Hubble Space Telescope

A telescope above the atmosphere that transformed our view of galaxies, nebulae and the expanding universe.

NASA / ESA / ASI

1997

Cassini–Huygens

Thirteen years orbiting Saturn; the Huygens probe made the first landing in the outer Solar System on Titan.

ESA

2004

Rosetta

The first mission to orbit a comet and land a probe, Philae, on its surface in 2014.

NASA

2006

New Horizons

Gave us the first close-up images of Pluto in 2015, revealing a heart-shaped glacier of nitrogen ice.

ISRO

2008

Chandrayaan-1

India's first lunar mission, which helped confirm the presence of water molecules on the Moon.

NASA

2012

Curiosity

A car-sized rover that found evidence that Gale Crater on Mars once held a long-lived lake.

ISRO

2014

Mangalyaan (Mars Orbiter Mission)

India's first interplanetary mission, reaching Mars orbit on its first attempt.

NASA / ESA / CSA

2021

James Webb Space Telescope

The largest space telescope ever launched, observing the first galaxies in infrared light.

NASA

2021

Perseverance & Ingenuity

A rover collecting Martian rock samples, and the first helicopter to fly on another planet.

NASA

2022

Artemis I

An uncrewed test flight of the Orion spacecraft around the Moon, paving the way for astronauts to return.

ISRO

2023

Chandrayaan-3

Made the first soft landing near the Moon's south polar region, with the Pragyan rover exploring the surface.

Years shown are the key year for each mission (launch, arrival or landing). Explore more at NASA Missions, ESA Space Science and ISRO.
