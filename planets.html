Three families

Rocky worlds, gas giants and ice giants

Terrestrial planets

Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars are small, dense and rocky, with solid surfaces you could stand on.

Gas giants

Jupiter and Saturn are huge balls of hydrogen and helium with no solid surface, dozens of moons and ring systems.

Ice giants

Uranus and Neptune contain more water, ammonia and methane "ices", giving them their blue-green colours.
