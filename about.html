Why we built Orbitra

Most of us first met the planets as a row of coloured circles in a textbook. That picture hides the most astonishing parts of the story — the vast distances, the tilted orbits, the storms bigger than Earth. Orbitra uses interactive 3D and scroll-driven animation to bring that scale back.

How we check our facts

Every number on the site — distances, orbital periods, mission dates — is compared against public data from NASA, the European Space Agency, ISRO and the International Astronomical Union. Where values change as discoveries are made, such as the number of known moons, we show a rounded figure.

Our principles

  • Free forever. No accounts, no paywalls and no advertising.
  • Accessible. Semantic HTML, keyboard-friendly menus and full support for reduced-motion preferences.
  • Lightweight. No frameworks or heavy libraries — the 3D effects are hand-written for speed.
  • Private. We don't use tracking cookies. Read our privacy notice.
